🟢FEATURE 01- *HTF FVG* (Up to 12 Timeframes)   
  
Displays Fair Value Gaps (FVG) from up to 12 different Higher Timeframes (HTFs) onto the current chart. The system features a strict mitigation rule: an FVG is only deemed mitigated and its status color changed when a candle on the respective timeframe closes entirely above (for a Bearish FVG) or below (for a Bullish FVG) the gap zone.   
Ensures High-Integrity Levels: This candle-close logic filters out noise from volatility-driven wicks or temporary liquidity sweeps, ensuring the trader is only acting on structurally validated, unmitigated institutional price magnets.  
  
Real-Time FVG Status Visualization Dynamically changes the color of the FVG box in real-time to clearly differentiate between active (unmitigated) and passive (mitigated) zones, using user-defined color schemes. Provides an immediate, precise visual signal of the current state of key inefficiencies, maximizing the probability of confluence-based entries.  
  
🟢FEATURE 02- *HTF PO3 Chart Overlay* (Up to 6 Timeframes)   
  
Plots consolidated HTF candles from up to 6 distinct timeframes directly onto the current chart. The display limit (e.g., up to 40 candles) and spacing are fully customizable. Enables Top-Down Confluence which offers a proprietary, structural view of the Power of 3 cycle on higher timeframes, allowing traders to seamlessly align short-term entries with macro-level accumulation or distribution phases.  
  
Also it projects the *FVG and Volume Imbalances* directly onto the overlaid HTF PO3 candles. Which Prevents Premature Execution & Provides crucial time-based risk management and granular liquidity context, preventing trades just before significant HTF candle closure events.  
  
🟢FEATURE 03- Session Liquidity & Killzone Boxes
